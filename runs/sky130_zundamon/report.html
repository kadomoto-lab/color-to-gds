Model assumptions and limitations

  • Public SKY130 nominal dimensions with planarization and empty M2-M5 approximations.
  • The 30 nm TiN top cap and optical properties are hypotheses; the drawing labels NILD3_C, not an identified TiN liner.
  • PI is lossless n=1.65; actual PI dispersion, absorption and metal alloy are not calibrated.
  • Metal fill, finite edges, oxide cladding differences and measured topography are omitted.
  • Infinite 1D periodic local solver; finite-pixel edges and neighboring-cell coupling omitted
  • Active region and guard region combined as an incoherent area average
  • No foundry signoff; density/fill/antenna and actual optical constants need process integration
